03 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
Let's clear them up.

A few quick answers about what Learnix is designed to do.

Who is Learnix for?＋

Learnix is designed for students who want a clearer way to organise study, practise topics and follow progress, including students following Pakistan's school curricula.

Can I change how the website looks?＋

Yes. Use appearance settings to switch light/dark mode and choose a theme colour that feels right for you.

Does Learnix replace teachers or textbooks?＋

No. It is intended to support your routine alongside classes, teachers, textbooks and other study resources.

Is Learnix free to use?＋

Yes. Learnix is free to use for students, with optional paid features available in the future.
